# Terrella — Privacy Policy

Hosting for the privacy policy of the **Terrella** Android app.

Live: https://dartvadius.github.io/terrella-privacy/

The page carries the policy in three languages — English (default), Ukrainian
and Russian — with a language switcher at the top.

Terrella is an interactive globe and solar system whose map works offline. It
has no accounts and collects nothing about its readers; the policy explains what
each network feature (Wikipedia descriptions, article translation and Live Earth
through our own server, Google Play purchases) actually sends.

`index.html` is generated from the markdown sources in the app repo at
`globus/publishing/privacy-policy/policy_{en,uk,ru}.md` — edit those, re-render
with `python3 tool/render_privacy_policy.py` (from `globus/`), and commit the
result here.
